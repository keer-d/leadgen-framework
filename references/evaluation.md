# Evaluating a Lead Generation Workflow

Volume is the easiest metric to inflate and the least useful metric on its own. Evaluate the workflow at several stages.

## Pilot Quality

- **Evidence completeness:** percentage of records with a working URL and a specific fit explanation.
- **Review acceptance:** percentage the human reviewer considers worth keeping.
- **False-positive rate:** percentage rejected for a known exclusion reason.
- **Unclear rate:** percentage that cannot be classified without more evidence.
- **Contactability:** percentage with a realistic, policy-compliant next step.

## Production Quality

- Required-field completeness
- Duplicate rate
- Broken or stale source rate
- Priority-label consistency
- Time between discovery and review
- Correction rate after production import

## Downstream Signals

When sales or partnership feedback is available, track:

- attempted contact;
- reply or engagement;
- wrong person or wrong category;
- useful conversation;
- qualified opportunity;
- reason for rejection or no fit.

Do not treat reply rate as a pure measure of discovery quality. It is affected by outreach, offer, timing, market, and sender reputation.

## Iteration Log

For every material rule change, record:

1. the observed problem;
2. the evidence or sample that revealed it;
3. the rule or process change;
4. the expected effect;
5. the next pilot used to test it;
6. whether the change improved quality or introduced a new failure.

This turns corrections into system memory instead of one-off cleanup.

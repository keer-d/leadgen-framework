# CFD / Forex / Broker lead generation example

This is an example industry adaptation for CFD brokers, Forex, Gold/XAUUSD, trading education, signal providers, EA/robots, copy trading, and IB/CPA affiliate partnerships.

Do not copy private client, company, CRM, or internal strategy into public versions.

## Example target profiles

High-directness targets:

- Signal providers with Telegram/Discord/WhatsApp groups.
- MT4/MT5 EA or robot sellers.
- Copy-trading teachers or account managers.
- Gold/XAUUSD, FX pairs, indices, commodities creators.
- KOLs showing trading results, platform screenshots, or lifestyle + trading funnels.
- Broker review/comparison/coupon sites.
- Trading tools: pip calculators, lot calculators, margin/risk tools.
- Trading education sites, courses, webinars, or academies.
- Web3/crypto IBs only when they overlap gold, commodities, indices, CFD, or broker CPA.
- Regulator/blacklist domains only after financial relevance is confirmed.

## Strong evidence

Product evidence:

- XAUUSD / Gold / Forex / CFD / indices / commodities.
- MT4 / MT5 / MetaTrader / cTrader / TradeLocker screenshots or tutorials.
- Broker execution, account opening, trade-copy, EA, VPS, or signal funnel.

Funnel evidence:

- Telegram/Discord/WhatsApp group.
- Link-in-bio, landing page, private community, contact form.
- Broker affiliate/referral links, IB code, coupon, CPA, RevShare, “partner” language.

Activity evidence:

- Recent posts/videos.
- Frequent market updates.
- Public screenshots, live sessions, pinned tutorials, community replies.

## Exclusions or down-rank rules

- Binary options-only.
- Prop firm challenge-only unless there is a clear broker/CFD funnel.
- TradingView-only accounts with no execution platform or contact/funnel.
- Crypto exchange-only accounts unless there is gold/commodities/indices/CFD overlap.
- Inactive accounts.
- Official institutional media if the goal is private IB/CPA/KOL partnerships.
- Off-topic blacklist domains such as hotels, restaurants, unrelated machinery, or generic parked domains.

## Example language rule

Use simple, final labels such as:

- `French`
- `English or Other`

Avoid leaving `To verify` in production. If uncertain, choose the dominant public language and explain the basis in verification notes.

## Example priority

- `P1`: direct signal/EA/copy-trading or broker takeover leads with strong audience and contact path.
- `P2`: smaller direct creators, hidden KOLs, or active communities with good fit.
- `P3`: education, tools, review sites, exploratory Web3/crypto overlap, or weaker-fit records.

# Synthetic Example: B2B SaaS Partners

> Fictional scenario. No real companies or partner records are included.

## Goal

Find implementation and referral partners for **Flowmark**, a fictional workflow-documentation SaaS for small operations teams.

## Target Profiles

- Boutique operations consultancies
- No-code and automation agencies
- Fractional operations leaders with active client work
- Communities that teach process design to small businesses

## Strong Evidence

- Public case studies involving workflow redesign
- Services pages mentioning implementation, automation, or process documentation
- Active educational content for operations teams
- A visible contact or partnership route

## Exclusions

- Generic software directories with no service relationship
- Agencies focused only on paid media or brand design
- Inactive websites
- Profiles that mention automation only as a keyword with no supporting work

## Priority Example

**P1:** active consultancy + relevant case study + reachable decision-maker + clear client overlap.  
**P2:** relevant services and evidence, but smaller reach or unclear partnership path.  
**P3:** educational community or adjacent tool that may support ecosystem research.

## Pilot

Collect 20 candidates across LinkedIn, agency websites, and partner directories. A human reviews every record before any CRM import.

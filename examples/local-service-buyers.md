# Synthetic Example: Local Service Buyers

> Fictional scenario. No real businesses or buyer records are included.

## Goal

Find multi-location businesses that may need **Northline Care**, a fictional commercial maintenance provider.

## Target Profiles

- Regional retail groups
- Small hotel groups
- Multi-site clinics
- Property and facilities managers

## Strong Evidence

- Multiple operating locations in the target region
- Recent openings, renovations, or expansion
- Public facilities or operations contact path
- Evidence that maintenance is centrally coordinated

## Exclusions

- Single-location consumer businesses below the service threshold
- Permanently closed locations
- Businesses outside the service radius
- Residential-only property listings

## Useful Channels

- Company websites and location pages
- Business registries and public directories
- Local development and opening announcements
- LinkedIn operations and facilities roles

## Pilot

Start with one city and 25 businesses. Verify every operating location and avoid collecting personal contact details that are not necessary for legitimate business outreach.

# Synthetic Example: Creator Partnerships

> Fictional scenario. No real creators or contact data are included.

## Goal

Identify potential educator partnerships for **LinguaLoop**, a fictional contextual language-learning product.

## Target Profiles

- Language educators publishing original lessons
- Study-system and memory creators
- Bilingual creators who explain real media or current events
- Newsletter writers with an engaged learner audience

## Strong Evidence

- Recent educational content with substantive explanations
- Audience questions that reveal a real learning need
- A public partnership or business contact route
- Demonstrated interest in tools, study methods, or contextual learning

## Exclusions

- Reposted content without original teaching
- Account activity driven mostly by giveaways
- Generic lifestyle creators with no learning connection
- Inflated audience signals without meaningful engagement

## Channel Differences

- YouTube: depth, teaching style, and durable content
- TikTok/Instagram: format fit and audience questions
- Newsletters: subject depth and direct audience relationship
- Communities: trust and recurring learner problems

## Pilot

Review 15 candidates across at least three channels. Separate creator fit from contactability so a strong educator is not incorrectly downgraded only because outreach is unclear.
